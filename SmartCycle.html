Expected Results: The App

Just keep your phone in your pocket or mounted on your handlebars. The SMART CYCLE app acts as your co-pilot, featuring:

  • ✓ Trained Road Anomaly Classifier: Automatically detects bumps and potholes.
  • ✓ Map-Matching & GPS Alignment: Accurately pinpoints the hazard on the cycle lane.
  • ✓ Share Photos: Users can manually add images to unverified hazards.

Data-Driven City Planning

Traditional inspections are expensive and manual, often leading to a lack of oversight for cycleways despite shrinking bicycle-centered budgets.

  • ✅ Zero Inspection Vehicles: Cut emissions and eliminate the need for physical patrol fleets.
  • ✅ Targeted Maintenance: Use real-time data to prioritize repairs and reduce accident risks.
  • ✅ Reduce Logistic Costs: Optimize maintenance schedules instantly.
